YOUR QUESTIONS / 009

Good questions.
Clearer answers.

A few things worth asking before committing to a new solar system.

?
How do I know what size solar system I need? +

It depends on electricity usage, roof space, shading, the orientation of the property, local network requirements and future plans such as an electric vehicle. An assessment should come before a recommendation.

Is a home battery worth considering? +

Storage may help you use solar generation outside daylight hours, but value depends on costs, usage patterns, electricity tariffs, battery performance and any available incentives. It is not the best fit for every property.

Can I add panels to an existing solar installation? +

Sometimes. Compatibility with the existing inverter, roof layout, electrical system, network approval and current standards must be checked before an expansion is proposed.

What is involved in a site assessment? +

A typical assessment considers the roof condition, usable area, orientation, shading, switchboard and meter arrangements, the household or business load profile and relevant installation constraints.

Can solar work with an EV charger? +

Yes, many EV chargers can be integrated with rooftop solar and suitable energy management equipment. The best solution depends on the vehicle, charging habits and compatibility of the electrical system.

Do you cover all of New South Wales? +

Service availability depends on the installer’s actual coverage. Contact the business with your suburb or postcode to confirm whether a consultation and installation can be arranged.
